Examples

These are parts of the tabs DeployLogs builds, filled in with its sample deploy: a made-up weekend cutover of a billing system across five teams. Column letters and row numbers are the ones in the sheet. Headings in orange are worked out for you; headings in beige are where you type.

The runlist

Steps from every team, in run order. Each step says what it waits for and what it hands over.

Before the deploy starts. On D-05, "Received from earlier steps" reads: D-04: Invoice and credit memo template IDs, for the integration release (not handed over yet).

Estimates

Plan hours use the longest practice run, times the data size factor. With no practice run, they use the owner's estimate.

The overview

Every number says where it comes from. Some rows are left out here.

Changes

Each change in the deploy, and whether it is covered.

Smoke tests

Every test names the person in the business who signs off the result.

Partway through business testing: T-01 and T-03 have been run. In the sample as filled in, every test starts as Not run.

Data migration

Record counts before, expected after, and loaded. Merging records can make the expected count lower.

The loaded counts are made up to show each result. The sample leaves them blank until the migration runs.

Teams

Every team is held to the same rule. A team with no steps at all shows No steps listed.

Audit log

During a run, only the person running it can edit, and the audit log records every edit.

These rows are made up to show what an entry looks like.
