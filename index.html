Rubber duck deploys.

Rubber duck debugging is talking your code through, step by step, until the problem shows itself. DeployLogs does that for a deploy: every team's steps in one runlist, walked through in a practice run before the night it counts.

A deploy runlist and go/no-go tracker for Google Sheets, for deploys that cross teams.

Deploys fail in the gaps between teams

One team runs sprints, another keeps its steps in someone's head. One system creates the IDs the next team needs, and nobody wrote that down. An estimate nobody has run becomes the plan. DeployLogs puts every team in one runlist, in the order it runs, held to the same rules.

One runlist across your deploy tools

Your deploy tools each cover one system. DeployLogs is the runlist across all of them, including the apps that can only be changed by hand.

A Gearset or Copado deployment is one step, with its link. Next to it sit the NetSuite field mapping, the Gainsight rule and the Outreach setting that no deploy tool reaches: in the order they run, with what each one waits for and how to roll it back.

What it does

One runlist, in order

Every step from every team, in the order the deploy runs, with what each step waits for and whether it can start.

See the runlist

Handoffs between teams

When one team creates something the next team needs, the value shows up on the step that is waiting for it.

Estimates with a source

Every estimate is marked Practiced, Owner's guess or Missing. Practice runs replace guesses.

Go or no-go

Each stage ends at a gate every team signs. The overview shows the latest time it is safe to keep going, with the rollback counted.

Smoke tests and sign-off

Every change has a smoke test and a named person in the business who signs off the result.
