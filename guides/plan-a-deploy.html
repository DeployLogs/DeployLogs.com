Guides

Plan a deploy

Set up the deploy: the changes, teams, stages, steps and gates.

Do this weeks before the deploy, with every team. Each team fills in its own steps.

1. Overview

Fill in Deploy name, Planned start and Must be finished by.

2. Changes

One row per change: a feature, a fix, a data model change. When you type its name, DeployLogs gives it an ID such as CHG-001. Fill in its Team and Business owner. Steps, smoke tests and record counts pick a change by its ID.

3. Teams

One row per team taking part, including a team that has not said what it needs to do yet. A team with no steps shows No steps listed.

4. Stages

Each stage has its own tab. A new tracker starts with Pre-deploy, Deploy, Data migration, Post-deploy and Business testing, in that Order. To add one, choose Extensions > DeployLogs > Add a stage.

  • Planned wait after (hrs): time after the stage that is not work, such as overnight or waiting for testers.
  • Point of no return: tick it when rolling back gets much harder once the stage is done.
  • What must be true to go on: what the gate checks.

5. Steps

Each team adds its steps to the tab for that stage. For each step: Change (ID), Team, Step, How to do it, Owner and Backup owner. The ID is set for you.

When you pick a change, Team fills in from the Changes tab. Change it if another team does the step. Leave Change (ID) blank for general steps, like a backup.

The order of the rows is the order they run: drag a row to move it, and do not sort the stage tabs. The Runlist tab shows every stage's steps in order.

Then the estimate, the waits and handoffs, and the rollback. Each has its own guide: Estimates and practice runs, Order, waits and handoffs.

6. Rollback for every step

On each step, say how to undo it in Rollback steps. A step without one makes its team Not ready.

Then write the rollback itself on the Rollback tab: its steps in the order they run, each with an owner and an estimate. A rollback is often not the deploy steps backwards. "Hours to roll back everything" on Overview adds up the Rollback tab.

7. Gates

On the Gates tab, add one row for every team that must say Go at the end of each stage: After stage and Team. On the day, each team fills in Call.

8. People and the rollback plan

List who is on the deploy and when they are available on People. On Rollback plan, write what counts as P0 to P3 for each system, what you do, and who decides.
